# Papers: space weather and heliophysics

Machine learning for the space environment that affects satellites and ground systems: solar wind and geomagnetic forecasting, coronal mass ejections (CMEs), and upper-atmosphere modelling. Many recent entries are preprints.

Thanks to [Victor Rodriguez-Fernandez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vrodriguezf90/) for his weekly arXiv picks on AI for space, which are a great source of papers for this list.

## Solar wind and geomagnetic forecasting
- AETHER-P3 Nowcast v1.0: Model Description, Training-Data Construction, and Validation: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06777)
- Beyond Mean Solar Wind Conditions: Turbulence-Aware Forecasting of the AE Index: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16518)
- Post-processing Probabilistic Forecasts of the Solar Wind by Data Mining Similar Scenarios: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11284)
- PISCES: Physics-Informed Solar-wind Convolutional autoEncoder for Space-weather Anomaly Detection and Early Warning: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28022)
