# Satellite and spacecraft vision datasets

- **SPEED** (2019): first public spacecraft pose estimation benchmark, synthetic images of the Tango spacecraft (PRISMA), used for ESA/Stanford SPEC2019. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.02050) · [ESA Kelvins](https://kelvins.esa.int/satellite-pose-estimation-challenge/challenge/)
- **SPEED+** (2021): next generation, focus on the synthetic-to-real domain gap. 59,960 synthetic images plus 9,531 hardware-in-the-loop images from Stanford SLAB's TRON facility. [Stanford repository](https://purl.stanford.edu/wv398fc4383) · [SLAB datasets](https://slab.stanford.edu/projects/datasets)
- **SPEED-UE-Cube**: dataset for autonomous vision-based spacecraft navigation, from SLAB. [Paper (AAS 24-027)](https://slab.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj25201/files/media/file/speed_ue_cube_a_machine_learning_dataset_for_autonomous_vision_based_spacecraft_navigation.pdf)
- **SPARK** (2021): about 150k images per modality (RGB and depth), 11 classes of spacecraft and debris; detection, segmentation, classification. Built with a Unity3D simulator. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.05978)
- **SPARK 2024**: trajectory estimation stream with 100 synthetic trajectories of 300 images and four real sequences (2,100 images) from the SnT Zero-G lab. [Challenge](https://cvi2.uni.lu/spark2024/)
- **Non-cooperative space target perception benchmark** (Scientific Data, large-scale synthetic). [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-06056-8)
