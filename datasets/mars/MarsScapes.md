# MarsScapes

First panorama dataset of unstructured Martian terrain for semantic and instance segmentation: 195 panoramas (3,779 sub-images), 9 classes: soil, sand, gravel, bedrock, rocks, tracks, shadows, background, unknown.
Paper: MarsScapes and UDAFormer (transformer-based unsupervised domain adaptation for Martian terrain segmentation).

Links: [GitHub](https://github.com/InRobots/MarsScapes) · [IEEE DataPort](https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/marsscapes-panorama-dataset-mars-terrain-understanding)
