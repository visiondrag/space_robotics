# S5Mars

6K high-resolution Mars images, sparsely annotated by confidence, built for semi-supervised segmentation. The paper proposes AugIN and SAM-Mix augmentations and a soft-to-hard consistency learning strategy (IEEE TGRS 2024).

Links: [Project page](https://jhang2020.github.io/S5Mars.github.io/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01200) · [Code](https://github.com/JHang2020/S5Mars_SSL) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Voxel51/S5Mars)
