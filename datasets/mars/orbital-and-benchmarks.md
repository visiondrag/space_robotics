# Mars orbital datasets and benchmarks (HiRISE, CTX, planning, DEM)

- **Mars-Bench**: 20 datasets, crater, cone, boulder, landslide, dust devil, frost, atmospheric dust, terrain classes. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24010) · [GitHub](https://github.com/kerner-lab/Mars-Bench)
- **MarsPlanBench / MoonPlanBench**: planar benchmark datasets for rover path planning, derived from high-resolution terrain imagery of Mars and the Moon. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21438)
- **MCTED**: machine-learning-ready dataset for generating digital elevation models from Mars imagery. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.08027)
- **HiRISE boulder segmentation** (from MOMO): polygon-annotated boulders by planetary scientists. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.02719)
- **CTX geomorphologic classification**: 15 landform classes in 5 thematic groups (aeolian, topographic, slope, impact). See [Mars-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24010).
- **RMaM-2020 rockfall dataset** (Moon and Mars): 484 positive and 330 negative Mars tiles. [Paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/frsen.2021.640034/full)
- **MARTIAN**: rendering framework producing aerial Mars imagery from HiRISE data. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.29647)
- Raw sources: [NASA PDS](https://pds.nasa.gov/), HiRISE and CTX images via PDS.
